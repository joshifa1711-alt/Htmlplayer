Keyboard notes maker v2

Fixed numbering: C=1, C♯=2, D=3, D♯=4, E=5, F=6, F♯=7, G=8, G♯=9, A=10, A♯=11, B=12

Insert:

Format: words (spaces between) | notes (spaces between). Example: Twin kle twin kle | C C G G
One word with several notes: write that word's notes together with no space (CC) or with a hyphen (C-C). Different words are separated by a space. Example: Twinkle twinkle little star | CC GG AA G or Twinkle twinkle little star | C-C G-G A-A G. Without any of these, each word gets one note. (The older way still works: a / between the notes of each word.)
Sharp: F# or F♯ · Flat: Bb or B♭ · Rest: - · High note: C' · Low note: G,
Only letters A to G are accepted. Do not type numbers or sargam letters; numbers are made for you.

No microphone? Tap the note you played (மைக் இல்லையா? வாசித்த note-ஐத் தொடுங்கள்):

Play one note at a time on a keyboard or any instrument near the microphone. The page marks each correct note and scrolls to it. Octave does not matter. You can also click any note box to jump there. (வாசிக்கும் note-ஐ page தானாகக் குறித்து scroll செய்யும்.)
